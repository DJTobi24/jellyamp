Jellyamp is a music player for your own Jellyfin server. It plays your library the way albums are meant to be heard: without gaps, at an even volume, with your artwork front and centre.

Jellyamp is not a streaming service and comes with no music. You need a Jellyfin server with a music library that your iPhone or iPad can reach, at home or over the internet.

PLAYBACK
• Gapless playback for albums and live recordings
• Crossfade between tracks, with a length you choose
• Equalizer with presets
• Loudness leveling, so quiet and loud tracks play at an even volume
• Plays your files in their original quality; the server converts only what iOS cannot play

YOUR LIBRARY
• Browse artists, albums, tracks, genres and playlists
• Recently added, recently played and favourites
• Fast search across the whole library
• Synced lyrics that scroll with the music, when your server has them
• Instant mixes and radio from any track

EVERYWHERE
• Download albums and playlists and listen offline
• Lock screen and Control Center controls, AirPlay
• Sleep timer
• Siri and Shortcuts: start your music by voice or from an automation
• iPhone and iPad

OPTIONAL
• Scrobble to Last.fm or ListenBrainz
• Add the free, self-hosted jellyamp-server next to Jellyfin for sonic similarity: tracks that sound alike, and mixes built from your own library

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Jellyamp collects no data. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. The app talks only to the servers you set up.

Jellyamp is free and open source. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with Jellyfin.
